Your media. Your choice.

This policy describes Kenuxa Video Studio, including its optional Kenuxa Watch community. Kenuxa is operated by Loshar Hammond. Contact losharhammond@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Downloads and file conversion are processed within your browser. Video and audio file contents are not uploaded to Kenuxa or its database. Download history, private source bookmarks, settings and an installation signing key are held in extension storage. Existing legacy personal video copies, if present, remain in local IndexedDB. New downloads save to disk and do not make an automatic duplicate video copy in browser storage.

Media detection while you browse

The extension observes media response URLs and headers on HTTP/HTTPS pages to identify supported files and playlists. Captured URLs, stream type, size and page/frame details are kept in browser session storage, capped at 100 sources per tab and cleared on navigation or tab closure. Scanning a page reads its video/audio source elements. The extension does not collect general page text, form inputs, passwords, cookies, payment data or advertising identifiers. Opening Studio from a page brings its title and URL into the local workspace.

When you share with everyone

Before a web download begins, you can choose Keep private or explicitly consent to Share with everyone. Sharing is not selected by default. After the download is confirmed saved, the extension sends the title and original public video webpage URL to the Kenuxa Watch service. You can also explicitly publish a discovery through the Watch form. Captured CDN manifests, signed stream tokens and downloaded video bytes are not published.

Shared titles, source links, publication dates and supported player details are public. A hash of the installation public key associates the listing with its publisher for removal. Signed requests also send a public verification key, timestamp, nonce and signature; the private signing key stays in the extension. Report reasons and the reporting installation hash are stored for moderation and are not shown in the public feed.

Services contacted

Kenuxa Watch uses Supabase for its metadata database and API, hosted in the project's EU region. The extension contacts that API when you browse the community, manage shares, publish or report. Supabase may process request IP addresses and operational logs as part of hosting. We do not sell data, run advertising tracking or include third-party analytics.

Downloading contacts the original media provider. Opening an embedded player contacts YouTube, Vimeo or Dailymotion; those providers control playback and may process viewing information under their own policies. Kenuxa loads supported players only after you open a video. Source links open the original website, which has its own privacy practices.

Retention and removal

Download history is limited to 100 entries and can be cleared in History. Private Watch source links can be removed from Saved privately. Shared by me lets you remove public listings created by this browser installation. Public listings remain until their publisher or the operator removes them. Related reports are deleted when the listing is deleted. Anti-replay nonce records are cleaned after ten minutes during subsequent service requests. Hosting logs follow provider retention settings.

Uninstalling or clearing extension storage removes local history, bookmarks and the signing key; it does not automatically delete public shares or downloaded files on disk. Losing the key also loses in-app management access to previous shares. For removal assistance, email the source listing URL or ID to losharhammond@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, private media access tokens or video files. The operator may request enough information to verify a removal request.

Your controls

You can keep every download private, use local conversion without public sharing, remove your shares, report listings and uninstall the extension. In Firefox, publication also requests the browser's optional data-collection permissions. Declining those permissions prevents publication. The extension has no paid subscription and no automatic video uploads.

Security and policy changes

Sharing requests use HTTPS and installation signatures. The database's privileged credential is held on the server and is not shipped in the extension. These controls do not guarantee uninterrupted service or prevent all abuse. Material changes to data handling will be reflected here and in the extension's disclosures before introducing a new use of data.
